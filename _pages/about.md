<h2 class="about-section-label" id="current-focus">Research Directions</h2>
<div class="about-section-content" markdown="1">

My research explores **self-evolving agentic systems** through an **IR-first, symbolic approach**. Rather than relying on unconstrained code generation, I develop structured, composable, and verifiable representations that enable agents to systematically construct and evolve their harnesses and shared environments.

My current work combines a **composable Context IR** for heterogeneous data and derived context, **agent-driven construction** of reusable tools, skills, memory, and context infrastructure, and **experience-driven evolution** of the system's own construction strategies.

The work connects [**agent memory and retrieval**](/publications/#memory-adaptation), [**test-time learning and serving**](/publications/#test-time-learning-serving), and [**semantic queries and vector search**](/publications/#retrieval-semantics). It builds on my earlier work in [hardware-efficient execution](/publications/#efficient-execution), [graph systems and learning](/publications/#graph-systems), and [query optimization and distributed analytics](/publications/#query-analytics).

My longer-term goal is **multi-agent and human–agent collaboration at scale** through trustworthy shared environments, so people and agents can build on one another's knowledge and work over time.

**Research perspective:** [Organizing the Shared Information World of Humans and AI Agents](/blog/2026/shared-information-world-ai-agents/) · [The Next Generation of Context Management](/blog/2026/next-gen-agent-en/)
</div>
</section>

<section class="about-section" aria-labelledby="selected-systems">
<h2 class="about-section-label" id="selected-systems">Selected Systems</h2>
<div class="about-section-content" markdown="1">

- [**AutoIA @ ByteDance**](/projects/1_autoia/) — builds **self-evolving agentic systems** that improve tools, skills, memory, and shared environments over time. An **IR-first approach** makes this evolution more structured, reusable, and verifiable.
- [**SEMA**](/projects/#sema) — brings **LLM reasoning into databases**, enabling SQL to analyze structured data alongside natural-language content. Achieves **2–10× faster semantic query processing** than three evaluated baselines.
- [**GES @ Huawei**](/projects/3_ges/) — a production graph database supporting **hundreds of billions of edges** and high-performance, real-time graph queries. Earned **#1 audited LDBC SNB Interactive results on both benchmark tracks** (2024 and 2025).
- [**TQEX @ Huawei**](/projects/2_tqex/) — accelerates **SQL analytics across heterogeneous AI hardware**, using portable tensor execution on NVIDIA and AMD GPUs and Apple chips. Achieves **12.2× over DuckDB and 22.7× over HeavyDB** on supported TPC-H SF100 queries.
