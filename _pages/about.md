<section class="about-section" aria-labelledby="selected-systems">
<h2 class="about-section-label" id="selected-systems">Selected Systems</h2>
<div class="about-section-content" markdown="1">

<ul class="about-systems-list">
  <li>
    <h3 class="about-item-title"><a href="/projects/#sema">SEMA</a> <span class="about-item-subtitle">— LLM-Powered Databases</span></h3>
    <p>Brings <strong>LLM reasoning into databases</strong>, enabling SQL to analyze structured data alongside natural-language content. Achieves <strong>2–10× speedups</strong> on evaluated semantic-query workloads.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3 class="about-item-title"><a href="/projects/3_ges/">GES @ Huawei</a> <span class="about-item-subtitle">— Large-Scale Graph Database</span></h3>
    <p>A production graph database supporting <strong>hundreds of billions of edges</strong> and high-performance, real-time graph queries. Achieved audited <strong>#1 LDBC SNB Interactive results on both tracks</strong>.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <h3 class="about-item-title"><a href="/projects/2_tqex/">TQEX @ Huawei</a> <span class="about-item-subtitle">— Hardware-Portable Database Acceleration</span></h3>
    <p>Accelerates SQL analytics across <strong>NVIDIA and AMD GPUs and Apple chips</strong> through portable tensor execution. Achieves <strong>12.2× speedup over DuckDB and 22.7× over HeavyDB</strong> on supported TPC-H SF100 queries.</p>
  </li>
</ul>

**#1 on both tracks of [LDBC SNB Interactive](https://ldbcouncil.org/benchmarks/snb/interactive/)** — [**Declarative**, 2024](https://ldbcouncil.org/benchmarks/snb/interactive/2024-09-16-graph-engine-service-sf300/) and [**Imperative**, 2025](https://ldbcouncil.org/benchmarks/snb/interactive/2025-12-01-graph-engine-service-sf300/) — including a reported **3,000× throughput advantage over the previous #2** on the declarative track.
